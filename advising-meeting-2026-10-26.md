Planning rules:
- Priority goes to courses required for the major; follow prerequisites; take courses in the order on the map.
- All freshman, sophomore and junior year courses should be done before SE 494/495.
- Electives and minors are secondary to major requirements, especially prerequisites.

- CS 124 counts in place of CS 101 (not vice versa for the CS minor).
- SFO/TO petitions: only 1 course below 300-level permitted (except the Computer Science SFO).
- Study abroad: best done by end of sophomore year; many 300/400-level IE/SE courses are offered only fall or spring.
